# Source reference

The default live input uses the National Park Service's official [General Grant National Memorial accessibility page](https://www.nps.gov/gegr/planyourvisit/accessibility.htm).

The proposed segment runs from the Riverside Drive sidewalk north of the overlook pavilion to the visitor center entrance via the ramp. The page distinguishes visitor-center access from the mausoleum and crypt. Do not generalize one supported entrance to the entire memorial.

The finalized `grant-visitor-center` record is included in [`evidence/studionet.json`](evidence/studionet.json), with the accepted source snapshot, segment finding and transaction references. The finding applies to that snapshot and the submitted segment. Access conditions and web availability can change.

The worked Northlight Museum example is fictional. Its source text, quotes and findings are illustrative; they are not attributed to a real museum or represented as chain evidence.
